### CMS Changes & Market Disruption Impact

- PPO plan exodus: CMS payment changes eliminated profitability
    
    - Massive national carrier withdrawal from PPO plans
        
    - Legacy plans with large membership going non-commission
        
    - California example: Central Health Plan, Molina all non-commission
        
- Flex card income counting changes:
    
    - Now counted as cash benefits for Medi-Cal/Medicaid eligibility
        
    - Affects utility assistance programs, housing share costs
        
    - Twice-yearly reporting requirement added
        
    - $250/month could disqualify from assistance programs
        
- SSBCI/VBID transition:
    
    - VBID discontinued by CMS (funded previous flex cards)
        
    - SSBCI criteria now required - proof of chronic condition needed
        
    - Each carrier files different SSBCI qualifications
        
    - Medical records/APS signatures required (claims insufficient)
        
- Zero copay elimination:
    
    - Returns to LIS level copayments
        
    - Even generics will have copays based on LIS tier
        
    - Members losing “spoiled” zero copay benefits
