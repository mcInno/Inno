### PACE Program Overview & Portal Training

- PACE = Medicare program for seniors with ADLs (Activities of Daily Living)
    
- Only enrollment method: [broker.innovage.com](http://broker.innovage.com/) portal (no Sunfire/Connecture)
    
- Eligibility requirements:
    
    - 55+ years old
        
    - 2+ ADLs in CA, CO, NM, FL, PA (some ADLs worth 2-4 points each)
        
    - Must live independently or room/board (not assisted living)
        
    - Safe, permanent residence required
        
- Three qualifier types: Medicare, Medicaid, VA (70%+ service-connected disability)
    
- Process mirrors life insurance applications
    

### Portal Features & Resources

- Back office access: enrollment status, commission statements, downline reports
    
- Sales & marketing resources:
    
    - ZIP code coverage maps with cities/counties
        
    - Co-branded flyers (English/Spanish)
        
    - Meeting/non-meeting promotional materials
        
    - Book of business letters (generic vs. meeting-driven)
        
    - Broker handbook with policies, commission details
        
    - CMS-compliant broker kits
        
- Provider directories include:
    
    - Internal PACE centers (primary care, podiatry, therapy)
        
    - Off-site specialists, dialysis centers
        
    - Skilled nursing, long-term care facilities
        
    - Hospital coverage: pays any hospital bill (no network restrictions)
        

### Application Process & Timeline

- Online application workflow:
    
    - ZIP code verification → $0 premium confirmation
        
    - Participant info + ADL assessment questions
        
    - Insurance details (Medicare/Medicaid numbers)
        
    - Submit → receive unique tracking ID
        
- Mandatory verification call (preferably at point of sale)
    
    - Confirms application accuracy
        
    - Extracts additional ADLs
        
    - Schedules intake assessment
        
- Full approval timeline:
    
    - Intake assessment: nurse visit for safety/health evaluation
        
    - IDT team review: 7 days average
        
    - State approval: ~30 days (CA), ~45 days (other states)
        
    - CMS approval: final step
        
    - Effective date: 1st of following month
        
- Current coverage remains active throughout approval process
