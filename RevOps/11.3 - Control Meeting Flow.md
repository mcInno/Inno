### Communication and Listening Model

- Neurolinguistic Programming breakdown:
    
    - Spoken words: 7% of what people hear
        
    - Credibility/expertise: 38%
        
    - Mirroring: 55% (most important)
        
- Mirroring techniques:
    
    - Match speaking pace and intellectual level
        
    - Adapt body language appropriately
        
    - Example: 88-year-old janitor vs CEO son requiring different approaches
        
- Question asking transformation:
    
    - Wrong: “Do you have trouble getting to appointments?” (Yes/no answer)
        
    - Right: “Tell me what it looks like to get to medical appointments today” (opens conversation)
        

### Meeting Control Framework - The 7 C’s

- **Create agenda** - who, what, when, where with 2/3 focus on learning their story
    
- **Confirm attendees** - roles, contact info, program eligibility
    
- **Communicate agenda** - ensure understanding and agreement
    
- **Confirm meeting** day before
    
- **Complete meeting** in agreed timeframe
    
- **Confirm next steps** - what happens, who owns it, when it gets done
    
    - Three critical questions for funnel control:
        
        - What has to happen to move person forward?
            
        - Who owns doing it?
            
        - When will owner complete it?
            
- **Communicate** contents to enrollment reps and meeting participants
