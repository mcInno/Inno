### Marketing vs Sales SMS Strategy Separation

- Strategic decision made to decouple marketing automation from sales experience
    
    - Marketing: Acceptable to use generic 800 numbers or short codes for automated messages
        
    - Sales: Requires personalized experience with individual rep phone numbers
        
    - Rationale: Different user expectations for automated vs personal communications
        
- Marketing SMS can proceed immediately with existing tools
    
    - No need to wait for complex sales integration solution
        
    - Standard appointment reminders and confirmations have different user expectations
        
    - Users expect automated messages from generic numbers (dentist offices, etc.)
        
- Sales SMS requires elegant solution maintaining personal relationship
    
    - Users expect consistent number for calls and texts with individual reps
        
    - Confusion arises when rep calls from one number but texts from different 800 number
        
    - Microsoft partner solution specifically addresses this requirement
