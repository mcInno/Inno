### Current Enrollment Pipeline Overview

- Three main outreach channels generating leads:
    
    - Field sales (75% of enrollments): Walk-in visits to senior centers, assisted living, hospitals
        
    - Broker team: 370+ contracted agents using e123 portal, anticipated growth from <5% to significant volume
        
    - Digital marketing: $4.5M annual spend on Facebook/Google ads generating form-based leads
        
- Contact center (10 FTEs) handles discovery and qualification for broker/digital leads
    
- Sales qualified leads scheduled for intake meetings with enrollment reps (40 total)
    

### Current CRM System and Handoff Process

- Four-stage approval system required for enrollment:
    
    - Enrollment stage (master control)
        
    - Financial (Medicaid eligibility)
        
    - Functional (LOC assessment)
        
    - HSD (health/safety determination)
        
- ERs conduct 1.5-hour in-home intake meetings
    
- ERNs currently do separate assessments via Word docs 2-3 days later
    
- Major participant complaint: asking same questions multiple times across visits
    

### Proposed CRM Improvements

- Consolidate intake information into single platform accessible by both ERs and ERNs
    
- ERNs can review/edit ER-collected data instead of starting from scratch
    
- Required field validation prevents scheduling without complete information
    
- State-specific forms and ADL requirements built into system
    
- Automated stage progression based on completed requirements
    

### Forms and Assessment Integration

- Electronic form completion replacing Word docs
    
- Three signing options:
    
    - Email-based signing
        
    - Mobile app finger signing
        
    - QR code scanning for paper forms
        
- Auto-population of participant data (name, DOB, social, etc.)
    
- Form storage tied to correct contact records and stages
    

### ERN Workflow and Scheduling Concerns

- Current inconsistent scheduling causes team frustration
    
- Proposed solutions:
    
    - ERs schedule nurses directly with calendar visibility
        
    - Queue system for multi-nurse centers (Lakewood, Denver, Aurora)
        
    - Bulletproof requirements prevent incomplete handoffs
        
- Self-scheduling preference vs. standardized process needs resolution
